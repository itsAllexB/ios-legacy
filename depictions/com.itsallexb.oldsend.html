Open this page on your iPhone or iPad.

  • OldSend 2.0 is my version of OldSend by lihaoyun6, the native LocalSend client for old iPhones, made for iOS 6 on Retina iPhones. It talks to the LocalSend app on today's phones and computers (Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS) over your Wi-Fi: no cables, no cloud.

    Share from any app with the OldSend button in the share sheet, watch every file on a live transfer screen, and turn on Run in Background to stay discoverable with the app closed.

Features

  • Works with LocalSend everywhere (protocol v2.2 over TLS 1.2), including sending to LocalSend on desktop
  • Live transfer screen: per-file progress and state, overall progress, files, size, speed and time left, with Cancel
  • OldSend in the share sheet of every app (Safari, Photos and more), and in "Open in…" for any file
  • Run in Background (off by default): discoverable and able to receive with the app closed; a notification opens the Accept / Decline prompt
  • Send photos, videos, text, the clipboard, links and any file, with a built-in file browser
  • Save to Photos: received photos and videos go straight to Photos, or stay in History
  • Network info on the Receive page, Quick Save, English and Simplified Chinese
  • Sharp on Retina screens, with a new app icon

Compatibility

  • Tested on iPhone 4S, iOS 6.1.3 (Cydia Substrate), with LocalSend 1.17 on Windows
  • iOS 6.x only (armv7)
  • Replaces lihaoyun6's OldSend (com.lihaoyun6.oldsend) if it is installed; settings and the device's LocalSend identity carry over
  • Settings

    In OldSend's own Settings tab:

    • Nickname: the name other LocalSend devices see.
    • Quick Save: accept incoming files without asking.
    • Save to Photos: move received photos and videos into Photos (default on).
    • Run in Background: stay discoverable and able to receive with OldSend closed (default off). When the phone sleeps, iOS pauses it, so it doesn't use battery.
