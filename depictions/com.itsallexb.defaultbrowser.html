DefaultBrowser (iOS 12)

DefaultBrowser is now DefaultApps.

DefaultBrowser has been replaced by DefaultApps (iOS 12), which also lets you choose the mail app and the maps app.

This version is an empty package: upgrading to it installs DefaultApps, which keeps the browser you picked. After that you can remove DefaultBrowser; DefaultApps stays installed.

Features

  • Installs DefaultApps (iOS 12) and moves your browser choice over
  • Contains no files of its own; safe to remove after the upgrade

Settings

None. The settings are in Settings > Default Apps.

Compatibility

  • iOS 12.x, like DefaultApps
