SafariScale

Smaller web pages in Safari.

Open this page on your iPhone or iPad.

  • Modern websites are made for phones that are much bigger. On a 3.5-inch screen they look cramped: big text, huge buttons and very little on screen at once. SafariScale shows pages at 80% of their size by default, or anywhere from 30% to 100%, so more fits on the screen.

    The page gets a real, wider layout and is shown smaller, so nothing is cut off or broken: links, forms, menus, pinch zoom and rotation all keep working. Works with Web Port (lululab's modern WebKit for iOS 6) as well as Safari's own engine.

Features

  • Page Size: 30% to 100% in steps of 10% (80% default, 100% is the iOS default)
  • Only pages made for phones change; desktop sites and fixed-width pages are left alone
  • A real layout: at 80% a page is laid out 400 px wide instead of 320 px, so the site's own CSS uses the room
  • Text stays in proportion: iOS text autosizing doesn't blow the text back up
  • Open pages update in place when you change the size; nothing reloads
  • Works with Web Port, including its own zoom controls, and with Safari's own engine

Compatibility

  • Tested on iPhone 4S, iOS 6.1.3 (Cydia Substrate), with Web Port 1.0.0 and with Safari's own engine
  • iOS 6.x only (armv7)
  • Only Safari changes; other apps and home-screen web apps keep the normal size
  • Settings

    Settings > Safari Scale:

    • Enabled: turns the tweak on or off.
    • Page Size: 30% to 100% in steps of 10%. 80% is the default.

    Pages that are already open update when you go back to Safari.
